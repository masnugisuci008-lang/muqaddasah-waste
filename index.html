How it works

Three taps to make waste visible.

1 · Snap

Take a photo of the waste near you.

2 · Pin

The app marks the place. Choose how bad it is.

3 · Send

Report it, with or without your name. The map updates for everyone.

Green · Handled

Waste is under control and managed by residents or local government.

Yellow · Watch

Waste is building up and needs attention before it gets worse.

Red · Critical

Waste is beyond a reasonable limit and is not being handled by the government or local residents.

Safe to report

Report the pile, never the person.

Anonymous option

Report without sharing your name or contact.

No blame

The map shows places, not people. It is not for naming or shaming anyone.

Privacy by design

Planned: faces and license plates in photos are blurred automatically.

Open and honest map

Everyone sees the same map, and the colors follow clear rules.

Roadmap

First we see. Then we eliminate.

Phase 1 · The first year

Reporting only

For one year, we focus on one thing: collecting reports so waste in every region becomes visible.

  • Photo and location reports
  • Green, yellow and red map
  • Anonymous and safe reporting
Phase 2 · After the first year

Waste handling

With the map as our guide, we start eliminating the waste that harms the world.

  • Controlled destruction by burning, in permitted facilities with emission controls
  • Organic waste treatment
  • Starting where the map is red

What we will not do: We do not turn plastic into recycled products. That only moves the problem around and the plastic never goes away. Our goal is to be the solution that destroys waste that cannot be handled and harms the world.
